## Assessment

- *(hard)* **2 of 6 people are under-utilised** — A. Kumar (41% of availability), R. Shah (36% of availability)
- *(hard)* **1 person is over-allocated** — M. Iyer (116%)
- *(derived)* **1 person sustained a six-day working pattern** — M. Iyer (10 weeks). Six-day weeks lose roughly 14% efficiency against 12% for five-day (Thomas & Raynar, JCEM 1997).
- *(derived)* **5% of logged hours fell outside business days** — 102h off-calendar (cost partial — some rates missing); concentrated in M. Iyer (67h), A. Kumar (7h), R. Shah (7h)
- *(hard)* **Forecast overruns plan by 31800** — planned 240000 / actual 198400 / forecast 271800
- *(derived)* **Billable ratio is 61%, but under fixed fee that is not the measure** — Hours spent erode margin directly, because capacity is a cost here rather than revenue. Track effort variance and cost-to-complete instead.

## Action plan

**1. Rebalance before you spend**

Move work from M. Iyer to A. Kumar, R. Shah. This is capacity you have already paid for, so reassignment costs nothing and protects margin. Peak demand currently exceeds availability for M. Iyer, so this is resource LEVELLING — expect the end date to move if the work cannot be absorbed (APM).

> In Zoho Projects: Workload report → reassign tasks, or bulk-reassign from the task list.

**2. Sustained overload is costing you output**

M. Iyer (10 weeks). Six-day weeks lose roughly 14% efficiency against 12% for five-day (Thomas & Raynar, JCEM 1997). This is a duration finding, not a busy week — a single heavy week carries no evidenced penalty, which is why this only fires on a sustained pattern.

> In Zoho Projects: Workload report → check availability and redistribute the recurring load, not just this week's.

**3. Forecast exceeds plan**

Forecast overruns plan by 31800 USD. Rebalancing above may absorb part of it; the remainder is a scope or budget conversation. No contingency percentage is suggested here — no standards body prescribes one, and AACE requires contingency to be derived from risk analysis rather than a flat uplift.

> In Zoho Projects: Project → Budget. Set a revised budget and enable variance alerts.

**4. Start a cost baseline of your own**

Zoho keeps no historical cost baseline — its baselines store schedule only, with no hours and no cost. Snapshot the budget figures on a schedule and you will own a genuine trend within a few weeks. Your own prior period is a better benchmark than any industry average.

> In Zoho Projects: Schedule a recurring snapshot of planned / actual / forecasted cost per project.

## What this does not cover

- Over 90% of entries are whole hours — time looks estimated rather than measured.
- 14% of time logs are unapproved — figures may move on approval.
- No cost rate for 1 user(s): T. Nair. Their effort is reported in hours only — cost figures exclude them.
- Billable ratio is not realisation. Realisation needs hours actually invoiced and collected, which lives in billing data rather than time logs. Read the ratio alongside invoicing before drawing conclusions about margin.
