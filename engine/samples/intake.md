# Cost and capacity assessment — self-reported

**Tracking time — Time is logged but people have no cost rates, so hours cannot become money.**

Confidence: indicative

## Assessment

- *(hard)* **Measurement maturity: Tracking time (level 1 of 4)** — Time is logged but people have no cost rates, so hours cannot become money.
- *(hard)* **Assumed working week: Monday-Friday, Friday half-day** — Changed on 3 January 2022 (federal government; the private sector largely followed). The weekend is Saturday and Sunday.
- *(derived)* **Under fixed fee, the metric that matters is effort variance and cost-to-complete** — Hours spent erode margin directly, because capacity is a cost here rather than revenue.

## Action plan

**1. Set a cost rate per person**

Give each person a cost rate. This single field is what turns hours into money, and nothing financial works without it.

> In Zoho Projects: Users → set a cost rate per person.

**2. Verify local working-time rules**

Working-time and overtime rules differ by jurisdiction and this assessment does not account for them. The relevant instrument here is the Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and its Executive Regulations. Confirm with whoever owns employment compliance before acting on any recommendation that changes hours.
