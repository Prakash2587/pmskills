How it works

From a question to a change in your project.

01Pick a skill

Each one covers a single job: capacity and cost, status reports, risks.

02Try it here

A guided tool turns your numbers into an assessment and a plan. No sign-up.

03Add it to your agent

Install the skill in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another agent you already use.

04Run it in Zoho Projects

The agent works on your real project data, and asks before it changes anything.

Skill gallery

Everything you need to manage your projects

A skill goes live only after it is checked against published standards and guidance. Each live skill names the ones it was checked against.

Its benchmarks have a source

Any figure it compares you against traces to a published standard or study. Rules of thumb nobody can trace are left out.

It shows its evidence

Each finding says whether it was read from your data, calculated from it, or is a judgement call.

It asks before it acts

It reads your project freely. Nothing changes until you say yes.
