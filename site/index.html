How it works

From a question to a change in your project.

01Assess

Give it your team and their week. It shows who is over capacity, what the week costs and where the budget is heading.

02Plan

It proposes specific moves. You edit, reorder or remove any of them, and every number recalculates.

03Run it in Zoho Projects

Your agent sets the plan up in your portal, reading the real figures first and asking before each change.
