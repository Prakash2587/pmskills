Cost & capacity assessment

Six questions. You get a graded assessment and an action plan you can download — no account, no email required.

What kind of team is this?

This decides whether a billable ratio means anything at all — and whether any published benchmark exists for you.

How is the work paid for?

This is the question most tools skip. Under a fixed fee, hours on a project erode margin — so the same number means the opposite of what it means on time & materials.

Where is the team based?

Sets the working week. Several regions don't use a Saturday–Sunday weekend, and assuming wrong turns an ordinary working day into phantom "weekend burn".

How many people deliver the work?

Delivery headcount, not the whole company.

Below about five, formal capacity planning usually costs more than it returns — we'll say so if that's you.

What do you measure today?

Be honest here. Each missing foundation closes off a class of question, and we'd rather tell you that than invent a number.

Anything else going on?

Optional, and it doesn't change the assessment — the analysis is deterministic. It goes to the product team as a feature request.
