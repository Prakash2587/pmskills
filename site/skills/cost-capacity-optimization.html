Cost & capacity optimization

Finds who is over capacity and by how much, proposes specific moves, forecasts the budget, and carries the plan out in Zoho Projects — asking before every change.

Use it

Ask a question

Start a new chat with the Zoho Projects connector switched on, and ask in your own words. For example:

Who on my team is over capacity this month, and what should move?
Where is the budget on my largest project heading, and why?
What share of last quarter's hours was billable, and is that the right measure for us?

Or bring a plan from the planner

  1. Build a plan in the planner and adjust it until it is the one you want.
  2. On its last step, press Copy the hand-off prompt.
  3. Paste it into your agent.

The agent then checks what your portal already holds:

  • Your people and work are already there — it reads the measured figures, shows where they differ from what you typed, and proposes each change.
  • The portal is new — it sets the plan up as projects and tasks, with owners and weekly hours.

If the plan uses generic names such as “Consultant #2”, it asks which real person each one is.

Safeguards

What it will and will not do

  • Reads freely, confirms every write. You see the exact change before it happens.
  • At most five changes per confirmation. Larger sets are done in batches.
  • Never writes a cost or billing rate. It lists the figures for you to enter.
  • Never adds or invites people. It tells you who is missing.
  • Never recommends removing a person or cutting someone's rate.
  • Says what it cannot see — and stops when the data is too thin to support a recommendation.
Basis

Where its rules come from

Before this skill went live, the figures and limits in it were checked against the published guidance below. A number that could not be traced to a source was left out, and judgement that is our own reasoning is marked as such.

What the skill doesWhere it comes from
Measures capacity against each person's real calendar, never a nominal weekPMI and AACE International guidance on resource calendars
Chooses between shifting work within its float and letting the date moveAPM, on resource smoothing and resource levelling
Flags overtime only when it is sustainedThomas & Raynar (1997), Journal of Construction Engineering and Management; EU Working Time Directive
Compares your utilisation with a benchmark only where a published one existsSPI Research, Professional Services Maturity Benchmark; Deltek Clarity A&E Industry Study
Reports a variance only against an approved baselinePMI
Reads the same hours differently for time-and-materials, fixed-fee and retainer workOur own reasoning, not a published standard — stated here so you can judge it

What it leaves out, and why

  • A target utilisation percentage such as 85%. Neither PMI's guide nor ISO 21502 sets a number, so you are compared with your own figures.
  • A standard contingency percentage. No standards body prescribes one; AACE asks for it to come from risk analysis.
  • Utilisation bands for agencies and managed-service teams. The figures in circulation trace only to vendor blogs.

This is independent work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by PMI, AACE International, APM or ISO. The full source list, with what each item was checked against, is in the repository's knowledge folder.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The agent says it cannot reach Zoho ProjectsRepeat step 02, then start a new chat with the connector switched on. The skill also walks you through this.
It answers without using the skillName it: “Use the cost-capacity-optimization skill.” Check both skills are listed in your agent.
Budget or workload figures are missingThose need Zoho Projects Premium; across projects needs Enterprise. The skill says which findings it cannot produce.
It reports hours but no cost for someoneThat person has no cost rate in Zoho Projects. Enter it there; the skill will not.
It refuses to analyse utilisationFewer than about four weeks of logged time. Setting a plan up still works.
