WBS builder

Breaks a project down into everything it has to produce, checks the structure, and creates it in Zoho Projects as a new project — after showing you exactly what it is about to create.

Use it

Bring a structure from the builder

  1. Build your structure in the WBS builder and finish its checks.
  2. On its last step, choose how it should map, then press Copy the hand-off prompt.
  3. Paste it into your agent.

The agent shows you a summary of what it is about to create — how many phases, task lists, tasks and subtasks — and waits for one yes. It then creates a new project in stages and reports back what exists.

Or start from a description

Start a new chat with the Zoho Projects connector switched on, and describe the project in your own words. For example:

Break down a warehouse fit-out into a work breakdown structure. We report by building element.
Build a WBS for rolling out a new CRM to three regional offices, then create it in Zoho Projects.

It asks how the project is governed before choosing a top level, proposes a numbered structure, checks it with you, and creates it only when you say so.

Safeguards

What it will and will not do

  • Always a new project. It never adds a structure into a project that already exists.
  • One approval for the whole structure, after showing you the counts. It stops at the first failure and tells you what exists.
  • Never assigns work to a role. Zoho Projects cannot; a task is assigned only when you name a real user of your portal, and is left unassigned otherwise.
  • Never invites people.
  • Never invents an estimate as fact. Any figure it supplies is marked as a sample until you confirm it.
  • Never judges completeness for you. Whether each branch holds everything it should is yours to confirm.
  • No dates. A WBS says what the work is, not when it happens.
Basis

Where its rules come from

Each check was traced to a published source before this skill went live, and what Zoho Projects can create was tested on a live portal. A rule that could not be traced was left out or is shown as a rule of thumb.

What the skill doesWhere it comes from
Asks you to confirm that each branch holds all of its work and nothing elseGAO Cost Estimating and Assessment Guide; US DOE WBS Handbook (the “100% rule”)
Keeps project management as its own branchGAO; NASA WBS Handbook
Expects at least three levelsGAO; US DOE
Gives each work package one accountable roleGAO
Asks what “done” means for each work packageGAO; NASA (the WBS dictionary)
Keeps dependencies beside the structure, not in itGAO
Bases the construction template on building elementsNIST, UNIFORMAT II (NISTIR 6389)
Bases the software rollout template on published structuresNASA’s information-technology WBS; the US defence business-systems WBS as reprinted by GAO
Allows either deliverables or stages at the top levelUS government guidance prefers deliverables; PMI’s guide (2000 edition) and APM allow others
Six levels of subtask, estimates on unassigned tasks, refusal of dependency loopsTested on a live Zoho Projects portal, November 2026

What it leaves out, and why

  • The “8 to 80 hours” rule. It appears in no standard that could be read, and ANSI/EIA-748 says work-package size varies with the project. You can set your own limit instead.
  • “A WBS must be nouns only.” Working schedulers dispute it.
  • A fixed maximum depth. No standard sets one; go as deep as you need to manage the work.

This is independent work, not affiliated with or endorsed by GAO, NASA, the US DOE, APM, NIST or PMI. PMI’s Practice Standard for Work Breakdown Structures, the current PMI guide and ISO 21511 are paid documents that were not read, so nothing here is attributed to them. The research notes are in the repository’s research folder.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The agent says it cannot reach Zoho ProjectsRepeat step 02, then start a new chat with the connector switched on.
It starts redesigning a structure you pastedTell it: “Create it as built. Do not change the structure.”
It stops part-way throughIt will say what exists. Fix the cause and ask it to continue, or have it move the new project to trash and start again.
Every phase has today’s dateExpected. Zoho Projects dates a phase on the day it is created when none is given. Set the real dates in Zoho Projects.
Tasks are unassignedThe person named for that role is not a confirmed user of your portal, or not a member of the new project. Add them in Zoho Projects, then assign.
Dependencies were not createdYour connection may have no dependency action. The agent lists them for you to add by hand.
